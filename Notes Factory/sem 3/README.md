# AI Use Cases — Simple Words

### 1. Climate Intelligence for Heatwave Monitoring

Uses **AI, weather data, and sensors** to monitor temperature, predict heatwaves, and give **early warnings** to people and authorities.

**Example:** If AI predicts extreme heat in the next few days, it can send an early heatwave alert.

***

### 2. AI Irrigation Advisory for Sugarcane

Uses **AI and field sensors** to check soil moisture, temperature, rainfall, and water availability and tell farmers **when and how much to irrigate**.

**Example:** If the soil is too dry, the system recommends watering the sugarcane crop.

***

### 3. AI Urine Test Strip Reader

Uses **color sensors or a camera and AI** to analyze the color changes on a urine test strip and provide **quick test results**.

**Example:** The system reads the color of the test strip and detects abnormal values.

***

### 4. AI-Based Inter-Satellite Data Routing

Uses **AI to automatically choose the best communication route** for sending data between small satellites and ground stations.

**Example:** If one satellite connection is unavailable, AI can select another available route.

***

### 5. AI Battery Thermal Management for EVs

Uses **temperature sensors and AI** to monitor an EV battery and control cooling to keep the battery at a safe temperature.

**Example:** If the battery becomes too hot, AI increases cooling to prevent overheating.

***
